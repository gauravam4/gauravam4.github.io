What I Do

Three ways I create value.

  1. 01

    AI-personalized commerce

    Purchase-propensity ranking, NLP review summaries, and product Q&A. I know when a classifier, an LLM, or RAG is the right tool — and when to build, buy, or partner.

  2. 02

    Experimentation-first releases

    Every feature is A/B tested and read in Adobe Analytics. Launches run from alpha to general availability, including iOS and Android features gated by dimension so rollout is not tied to the app release.

  3. 03

    Prototype-as-PRD

    I replace the static requirements doc with a working prototype — Cursor, Emergent, Git, Render — and automate the surrounding workflow with n8n and Zapier.
